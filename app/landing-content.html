FAQ

Common questions about FidelityPlus, how it works, and what it can do for you.

Is my data secure?

Yes. FidelityPlus uses end-to-end encryption to protect your financial information. Only you can access your data. We never sell or share your information with third parties.

Can I use it offline?

Yes. FidelityPlus works without internet. Your data syncs automatically when you reconnect. This means you can track expenses anywhere, anytime.

Does it work on all devices?

Yes. Access FidelityPlus on your phone, tablet, or desktop. Changes sync instantly across all devices so you're always up to date.

Can I import my bank data?

Yes. Upload bank statements or connect your accounts directly. FidelityPlus parses the data and categorizes transactions automatically using AI.

How does the AI categorization work?

The AI learns from your spending patterns and merchant data to sort transactions into needs, wants, and debts. You can adjust categories anytime to match your preferences.
